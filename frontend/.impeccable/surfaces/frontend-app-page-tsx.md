Scope: the public landing route (`frontend/app/page.tsx`), the only Persuade
surface in a product that is otherwise Operate. It opts out of the app shell and
renders full-bleed.

Audience and job: a B3 retail or prosumer investor who already believes
backtesting, portfolio tracking and IRPF are three separate products, because
that is how the market sells them. The action is `Começar grátis`; the belief
that has to land first is that all three are one calculation here.

Proof available: the product is pre-launch — no users, no testimonials, no
logos, no usage metrics. The only proof this page can honestly offer is
mechanical: the real B3 extract schema, the parser's 2 report kinds and 16
movement labels, and an apuração chain the reader can check with a calculator.
Every figure is either traceable to the parser or labelled `exemplo`.

Chosen direction: **the engine in cross-section**. Four stages clamp onto one
vertical signal path — Entrada (the B3 extract), Apuração (the core, a closed
arithmetic ledger ending in a DARF), Saídas (the same apurado figure surfacing
in Bench, Carteira and IRPF), Ciclo (the fiscal year, its one-month DARF offset
and the Jan–Mai declaração window). Planos and the close sit off the path.

Memorable moment: one figure — R$ 2.281,20 — marked wherever it reappears, so
the claim "the same engine" is demonstrated rather than asserted. The mark is
the same navy tint the active nav item uses: the accent naming a location.

Refused: the SaaS stack this page used to be — a stats band, four icon cards,
three alternating text/visual rows, three numbered steps. Also refused: a
decorative ticker-tape marquee of hardcoded quotes, which asserted market data
the page does not have.

The hero does now carry a second surface over the extract sheet — the app's
posições table, offset and shadowed. This is not the floating dashboard card
that was refused, which was decoration: a generic product shot hovering on a
gradient. This one is an argument. Its tickers are the extract's tickers, each
last price is the price on the sheet underneath, and every resultado and both
totals close by hand — so the overlap says *this sheet becomes this readout*,
which is the page's whole claim, made before the argument starts. Labelled
`exemplo` like every synthetic figure here.

Motion: one authored moment, systematised — a stage entering view lifts its
content while its node on the signal path fills and any chart inside it draws
once. No entrance animation is scattered per element.

Unresolved: no live element ties the page to the real calendar (a "you are
here" marker on the year strip was considered and dropped — it needs a client
component under static export and it foregrounds deadline anxiety over the
mechanism). Revisit if the seasonal Jan–Mai acquisition push needs urgency.
